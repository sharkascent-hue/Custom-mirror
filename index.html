    Can I order a mirror cut to any size?

    Yes. Every mirror is cut to your exact dimensions. Enter your width and height in our mirror calculator, choose your options and the price updates instantly. The largest single sheet is 2400 × 1300mm.

    Do you offer glass cut to size?

    Yes — toughened glass for shelving, tabletops, balustrades, shower screens and more, all made to measure with polished edges. Price your glass.

    Can I get an instant quote online?

    Yes. Pick a product, enter your dimensions and the price updates automatically as you type, with the area shown in m² next to it.

    How long does delivery take?

    Orders are delivered within 14 working days. We cut all our own glass in-house, which keeps lead times reliable.

    How much is delivery?

    We deliver to the whole of UK mainland. Delivery costs are added at checkout, based on your order and location.

    Which thickness should I choose?

    6mm is perfect for bathroom and gym mirrors. 4mm is better for smaller framed mirrors and is available in silver only.
